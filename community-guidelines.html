Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 20, 2026

FundB aims to create a welcoming community for people around the world.

  • Respect other members; do not threaten, bully, harass or discriminate.
  • Do not impersonate staff, create deceptive offers or promise guaranteed earnings.
  • Never request or share passwords, one-time codes, private keys or recovery phrases.
  • Do not spam, use bots, manipulate referrals or exploit reward systems.
  • Share lawful, original content and respect intellectual property.
  • Report suspicious messages and scams to official support.

Moderation, blocking, reporting and group controls are not yet fully implemented in this preview. Avoid sharing sensitive information.
