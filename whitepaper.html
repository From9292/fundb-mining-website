A public overview of the FundB project, its current preview and the development direction.

1. Our vision

FundB Mining is a free-to-join community project focused on digital learning, responsible exploration of blockchain technology and genuine community participation. We want people from different countries and backgrounds to have a clear place to learn, follow project progress and contribute as the platform develops.

Our guiding principles are transparency, fair participation, useful learning, privacy, security and realistic communication. We will distinguish clearly between features that are available now, features being tested and ideas that remain on the roadmap.

2. What the current preview offers

The current FundB preview supports account registration and sign-in, a member profile, referral attribution, project information and selected community contribution workflows. Availability may vary while the service is improved. A displayed screen or balance is not proof that a payment or token has been issued.

FundB points are internal participation records only. They are not money, a stablecoin, an investment, a deposit or a claim on project funds. They have no guaranteed conversion rate or redemption value.

3. Fair participation and community integrity

FundB is designed around one personal account per participant. Referral attribution and mission submissions help the project understand genuine community activity. Any future reward programme will require clear eligibility rules, abuse prevention, review procedures and a secure record of qualifying activity.

A shared phone, household network or public internet connection alone should not be treated as proof that two accounts belong to the same person. Where an account needs review, participants can contact support through official channels. Bots, duplicate reward claims, impersonation and deliberate manipulation may be restricted under published rules.

4. Learning and useful contributions

FundB aims to publish practical material about blockchain basics, testnet and mainnet differences, digital account safety, responsible participation and project development. Community missions are intended to encourage genuine feedback and learning—not empty clicks, automated activity or misleading claims.

Participants should never share passwords, one-time codes, private keys or wallet recovery phrases. Support will never need those secrets.

5. Token and testnet direction

The project is working toward a proposed FUNDB token launch target in December 2028. This is a roadmap target, not a guaranteed launch date, listing, market price, return or token allocation. Network, contract, supply, distribution criteria and claim process will be published through official channels only when finalized and ready for independent verification.

Testnet environments are used for development and contract testing. Testnet assets are not live mainnet FUNDB and should not be treated as money. FundB does not currently distribute on-chain testnet tokens to app users.

6. Future community distribution

FundB may consider verified participation records under a future community distribution programme. This does not guarantee an airdrop, allocation, conversion or 1:1 migration. Before any programme begins, FundB will publish its eligibility period, qualifying activities, snapshot method, anti-fraud controls, exclusions, review and appeal process, allocation method and claim instructions.

7. Mining and reward concepts

FundB Mining is intended as a community participation and rewards feature, not proof-of-work cryptocurrency mining. Earlier design discussions included possible session and ad-eligibility concepts. Those figures are provisional ideas only: they may change or be cancelled and do not create an entitlement. Mining and reward crediting remain disabled.

Any future rewarded-ad feature must use an advertising provider and format that expressly permits the proposed incentive, with server-side completion verification, anti-fraud controls and final published terms. Ordinary display advertisements, including AdSense ads, are separate from participation and never earn points or mining progress. Users must never be asked to click ads.

8. Wallet and transaction safety

The wallet area is a development placeholder. Real wallet creation, deposits, transfers, swaps and withdrawals are disabled. They will not be activated until secure key storage, clear transaction review, recovery safeguards, testing and independent security review are complete. Never send funds to an address received through unofficial messages.

9. Sustainability and advertising

FundB may explore approved non-incentivized advertising, clearly labelled sponsorships and partnerships to support operating costs. Advertising approval, ad serving and revenue depend on provider review, genuine traffic and applicable policies. Project revenue is separate from member points and any future token assets.

10. Privacy and participant protection

FundB uses third-party infrastructure for account and backend services. The privacy notice explains the information used, service providers, advertising technologies and how to contact the project. The notice and terms may be updated as services and supported locations expand. Participants should review them before using the platform.

11. Development roadmap

  • Community experience: improve registration, account access, project updates, support and referrals.
  • Learning and media: publish useful original guides and clear progress updates.
  • Participation records: improve mission review, transparent rules and anti-abuse safeguards.
  • Rewards readiness: complete compliant provider integration, signed verification and secure ledger testing before considering activation.
  • Wallet readiness: complete secure key protection, transaction controls, recovery design and independent review before enabling real transactions.
  • Token preparation: research technical, operational and legal requirements toward the proposed December 2028 target.

12. Official updates and contact

Material changes to eligibility, rewards, token distribution or wallet functionality will be communicated through official FundB channels before participants are expected to rely on them. Please be cautious of impersonators, unofficial token claims and guaranteed-profit messages.
