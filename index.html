About FundB Mining

FundB Mining is a free-to-join community project focused on digital learning, participation and responsible exploration of blockchain technology. Members can create an account to access the FundB community interface, review project updates and follow the development roadmap. FundB is a developing project; features are introduced only when their supporting services and safeguards are ready.

The project is working toward a proposed FUNDB token launch target in December 2028. This is a development target, not a promise that a token will launch on that date, have a market price, or provide a financial return. Testnet assets and points displayed during the preview are not cash, deposits or withdrawable cryptocurrency.

How the FundB preview works

  1. Create an account using an email address you control, then sign in to the FundB application.
  2. Review the project information, learning materials, community features and participation tasks available in the preview.
  3. Keep your account password and verification codes private. FundB support will never ask for a wallet recovery phrase or private key.

Some screens describe planned features. A screen or displayed balance does not mean that mining, reward crediting, token distribution, deposits, transfers or withdrawals are active. The current preview does not promise earnings.

Points, mining and wallet status

FundB points are community participation records for the preview. They are not money, a stablecoin, an investment, or a claim on funds. They do not have a guaranteed conversion rate or redemption value. The BNB Smart Chain testnet is used for development and testing only; testnet tokens are not mainnet FUNDB.

Mining and reward crediting are not active. Any future rewarded-ad programme must use an advertising provider that permits the proposed reward format and must verify completions on the server. Ordinary display advertisements are separate from participation and must never be clicked or viewed as a condition for receiving points.

The wallet area is a development placeholder. Real wallet creation, deposits, transfers, swaps and withdrawals remain disabled until secure on-device key protection, transaction controls, testing and independent security review are complete. Never send funds to an address shown in an unofficial message.

Learn, build and grow with FundB

Digital technology is easier to explore when people can learn together. FundB is building a practical learning space for newcomers and experienced community members. Start with these original guides:

Blockchain in plain language

A blockchain is a shared record maintained by a network of computers. Learn how transactions are grouped, why networks use validators or miners, and why a public ledger is different from a bank account.

Testnet versus mainnet

A testnet is a practice network for developers to test applications and smart contracts. Testnet tokens are for testing only; they are not the same as a live asset and should never be treated as money.

Protect your digital account

Use a unique password, enable available account protections, check website addresses before signing in, and never share a recovery phrase, private key or one-time verification code. Real support teams do not need these secrets.

FundB Mining — Whitepaper

VERSION 1.3 · NOVEMBER 2026 · DECEMBER 2028 TARGET

Join a global community. Learn, contribute and build toward the FundB ecosystem together. This is a development whitepaper describing current capabilities and future proposals—not a financial offer, promise of earnings, token allocation or guaranteed airdrop. Planned features and draft figures may change. Only final rules published before a programme begins will apply.

1. Introduction — Participate today, build toward tomorrow

FundB Mining is a community participation platform created to bring people from around the world together through digital activities, learning, community missions, referrals and the growing FundB ecosystem. Our vision is to make participation accessible: anyone eligible should be able to join without paying to participate in planned community reward activities.

FundB is building toward a future FundB Token launch targeted for December 2028. This target represents our ambition and development roadmap, not a guaranteed date. As FundB grows, we intend to recognize genuine members whose real participation and support help build the community.

2. Our vision and commitment

FundB uses a one-person, one-account participation principle to support a fair community. Members should register and participate through their own account. Account-integrity checks may consider multiple signals, including unusual registration and reward patterns. A shared device alone is not proof of duplicate ownership. Where a concern needs review, the member may contact support for a human review. Any restriction will be proportionate and handled under the published programme rules.

We want FundB to grow into an accessible global community where genuine participation, learning, contribution and community support matter. Our guiding principles are free participation in eligible activities, fair verification, transparent rules, respect for participants and protection against abuse.

FundB is being developed step by step. We will communicate progress, publish programme rules and improve the platform as the community and infrastructure grow.

3. FUNDB community balance

FUNDB balances are community tracking units only. They do not represent cash, a market value, a token allocation or a promise of payment.

4. The FundB Token vision

The long-term goal is to prepare a FundB Token ecosystem that may recognize eligible genuine community participation. The intended target is December 2028. Token technical details, network, contract, supply, distribution rules and claim process will be published through official FundB channels when finalized and ready for verification.

FundB will work toward a transparent and responsible token design, with published criteria and safeguards. Participants should rely only on announcements from official FundB channels.

5. Participation and community reward direction

FundB's intended community approach is to recognize genuine participants who contribute to the growth and support of the platform. Subject to final published programme rules, eligibility and verification, rewards are intended to be available to qualifying genuine participants across the community—not reserved solely for a small group of insiders.

The reward framework, eligibility period, activity measures, allocation and distribution method must be published before any reward distribution. Genuine activity matters: duplicate accounts, bots, fraud, manipulation or abusive activity may be excluded under those rules.

6. Testnet, participation records and future distribution

The deployed BNB Smart Chain testnet contract is a development and testing environment. Testnet FUNDB is not the live mainnet asset, has no established market value and must not be treated as a payment or investment. The current FundB app does not distribute on-chain testnet tokens to users, and mining/reward crediting remain disabled.

FundB may preserve verified account and participation records for consideration under a future mainnet community distribution programme. This is not a promise of an airdrop, token allocation, conversion or 1:1 migration. Before any distribution, FundB will publish the eligibility period, qualifying activity, snapshot method, anti-sybil and fraud controls, exclusions, review/appeal process, allocation method and claim steps. Only the final published rules will govern eligibility.

7. FUNDB Mining

FundB Mining is a community rewards programme, not proof-of-work cryptocurrency mining. An early design concept has discussed 3 FUNDB per verified 24-hour session and an ad-eligibility step. These figures are provisional planning ideas only: they may change or be cancelled and do not create a reward entitlement. Mining and reward crediting are disabled until a compliant provider, server verification, anti-fraud controls, a secure ledger and final published programme rules are ready and tested.

8. Missions and meaningful activity

FundB missions are intended to encourage learning, feedback, safety awareness and helpful community contributions. Members can currently submit mission evidence for review. Submissions are recorded as pending review; mission submissions are currently saved for review. FUNDB rewards will require published eligibility rules, verification and secure recordkeeping before activation. Future eligible mission rewards will require published criteria, verification and secure recordkeeping.

9. Referrals and community growth

Inviting friends and introducing new genuine members can help FundB grow. Referral records support community tracking and integrity. Any referral-related reward eligibility, limits and verification requirements will be announced in official programme rules before reward crediting or distribution begins.

10. Wallet and distribution

The wallet and token distribution functions are part of the future development direction. Transfers, withdrawals and token claims are not currently active. If a distribution is introduced, FundB will publish official instructions, supported networks, eligibility, fees, timelines and verification details. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

11. Sustainability

FundB plans to explore suitable advertising, partnerships and other sustainable sources to support platform development and community operations. Revenue, reward funding and timing depend on development, operational conditions and applicable rules. Members should never click ads or engage with advertisements in ways that violate an advertising provider's terms.

12. Security and participant protection

FundB is developing authenticated accounts, secure submissions, review controls and safeguards against abuse. The platform continues to improve and is not represented as having completed an independent security audit. FundB will not ask participants to disclose passwords, recovery codes, private keys or seed phrases to claim rewards.

13. Roadmap toward December 2028

  • Community platform: improve account experience, app access, referrals, missions and support.
  • Participation infrastructure: develop secure point records, activity verification and anti-abuse safeguards.
  • Community expansion: improve languages, content, media and engagement tools.
  • Reward programme: publish verified eligibility and reward rules before enabling crediting or distribution.
  • Token readiness: research, build, test and prepare the technical, operational and legal framework toward the December 2028 target.

14. Transparency

FundB intends to publish material programme updates and changes through official channels. Reward criteria, distribution information and relevant technical details will be communicated before participants are expected to rely on them. Completed milestones will be distinguished from planned features.

15. Growing responsibly, together

FundB is building a community where genuine participation, consistency and support can help shape the project's future. We welcome eligible participants from around the world to join for free, explore the platform, take part in community activities and follow our progress toward the December 2028 FundB Token launch target. As the project grows, we will share official updates, clarify participation criteria and introduce new features in stages. Community trust, fairness and long-term growth are central to our vision. Please use official FundB channels for announcements and never share private keys or recovery phrases.

16. Join the journey

FundB is building a global community ahead of its targeted December 2028 token launch. Join for free, explore the platform, take part in activities as they become available, invite genuine community members and follow our progress. We want the journey to be open, engaging and meaningful—and we will publish official programme rules and progress updates as FundB develops.
