How FundB Mining works

1. Create and verify an account

Register and sign in with an email address and password. Email verification and additional sign-in providers may be added after configuration.

2. Complete rewarded ads

Complete 10 eligible rewarded advertisements. The ad provider verifies each completion; ordinary ads must not be incentivized.

3. Start a 24-hour session

A verified session runs for 24 hours. The server records the start and end times so closing the app does not erase progress.

4. Earn points and complete tasks

Eligible activity is recorded in a secure ledger. Tasks are subject to verification and published rules.

5. Token distribution preparation

Before any token distribution, the project will publish eligibility, anti-fraud rules and the points-to-token formula. Points do not guarantee tokens.

FundB Mining & Media — Whitepaper

PROJECT VISION · NOVEMBER 2026 · PROPOSAL

Executive overview

FundB is an early-stage global participation ecosystem designed to unite learning, community contribution, creativity and digital participation through a mobile-first experience. The project aims to build a useful community before any token launch: accessible tools, transparent rules, measurable contributions and a responsible approach to security and sustainability. FundB is under development; its future and any token value are uncertain.

Mission and principles

Our mission is to make meaningful digital participation accessible across regions and languages. We prioritize honest communication, inclusive design, privacy, useful learning, community safety and verifiable records. The project will not present preview balances as money or promise profits.

Platform architecture and features

The planned platform includes secure accounts, a community profile, FundB Media, structured learning missions, task experiences, achievements, platform statistics and rankings based on verified activity. Additional features may include moderated groups, messaging, creator tools and games, subject to development, testing and safeguards. Features may be delayed, changed or cancelled.

Proof-of-Skill Passport

The proposed passport is a progression record built from learning challenges, knowledge checks and contributions that can be independently verified. Levels and badges will recognize participation and demonstrated learning rather than repetitive clicks. Submissions may require review and appeal procedures. A badge or level is not a credential from an accredited institution and does not guarantee points or token eligibility.

FundB Points and illustrative reference

FundB Points are proposed internal participation units in the preview. They are not presently cryptocurrency, cash, securities, deposits, transferable assets or a promise to pay. No redemption mechanism or fixed value currently exists. Any illustration such as 10 points shown alongside a $2 reference is hypothetical only and must not be interpreted as a cash balance, guaranteed conversion, token price, entitlement or offer. Actual reward rules, if activated, will be published before use.

Participation and eligibility

Future participation activities may include learning missions, knowledge challenges, contribution reviews and approved community tasks. Eligibility may require account security, anti-fraud screening, regional availability, age and identity checks where lawful, and compliance with published rules. Bots, duplicate accounts, manipulation, false evidence and abuse may be disqualified. Users must not be asked to pay to unlock promised rewards.

Statistics and rankings

Statistics will be published only when supported by reliable backend data. Rankings may use verified contribution, mission completion or learning progression and may offer weekly and monthly periods. Privacy-protective display names, moderation, anti-cheating controls, exclusion of sensitive data and a correction or appeal path are required before live rankings.

Token vision and possible distribution

The project may explore a token after product development, legal assessment, technical security review and governance decisions. The chain, token standard, supply, allocation, vesting, liquidity, contract address and launch date are not final. The 2028 target is tentative and may change or be cancelled. No airdrop allocation, exchange listing, token price, liquidity, profit or future value is guaranteed. Participation points may never convert to tokens.

Identity verification and withdrawal controls

Before any future withdrawal or distribution, the project may require identity verification and compliance checks through a suitable provider, subject to privacy law, regional eligibility, data minimization, secure handling and an appeal process. Verification is not currently active, and no withdrawal or token distribution is enabled. The project must disclose the provider, data use, retention, access controls and user rights before collecting identity documents.

Long-term sustainability

Sustainability will require useful products, a transparent budget, lawful and policy-compliant revenue, careful token economics and responsible stewardship. Possible revenue sources may include ordinary non-incentivized advertising, clearly labeled sponsorships, optional creator services or other lawful models. Revenue is not currently established. Ordinary advertising must never be tied to clicking ads or earning points. A stable token value cannot be guaranteed by this project; any stability mechanism would need a separately disclosed design, backing, controls, legal review and credible reserves, and is not proposed as an existing feature.

Security, governance and risks

Authentication and cloud profiles currently use Appwrite. Secure reward ledgers, independent audits, anti-abuse systems, backup and recovery, rate limits, access controls, moderation, incident response and compliant integrations remain prerequisites for production rewards. The project is experimental and may face operational, legal, security, market, liquidity and execution risks. Never share credentials or pay an unofficial person to guarantee participation or allocation.

Roadmap

Stage 1: community preview and account experience. Stage 2: reliable verified mission records, privacy-safe statistics and progression. Stage 3: moderation, content and community tools. Stage 4: security, legal and economic review. Stage 5: evaluate a possible token launch and distribution only after readiness gates. This roadmap is indicative, not a commitment.
