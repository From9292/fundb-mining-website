How FundB Mining works

1. Create and verify an account

Register with email and password or Google sign-in once a secure authentication provider is connected.

2. Complete rewarded ads

Complete 10 eligible rewarded advertisements. The ad provider verifies each completion; ordinary ads must not be incentivized.

3. Start a 24-hour session

A verified session runs for 24 hours. The server records the start and end times so closing the app does not erase progress.

4. Earn points and complete tasks

Eligible activity is recorded in a secure ledger. Tasks are subject to verification and published rules.

5. Token distribution preparation

Before any token distribution, the project will publish eligibility, anti-fraud rules and the points-to-token formula. Points do not guarantee tokens.

FUND-B Project Whitepaper

VERSION 1.0 · NOVEMBER 2026

1. Overview

FundB Mining is a proposed community rewards platform. It begins with internal demo points and aims to explore a blockchain token ecosystem with a target launch in 2028. The launch and distribution are conditional, not guaranteed.

2. Vision

Build an accessible, transparent rewards community with clear rules, secure accounts and responsible growth.

3. Participation

The proposed experience includes account verification, qualifying rewarded ads, 24-hour reward sessions, daily activities and referrals. Reward rates and task eligibility will be published before production use.

4. Points and token distinction

FUND-B points are internal accounting units in the prototype. They are not cryptocurrency, cash, a deposit, or a promise of future tokens. A conversion formula has not been finalized.

5. Wallet and blockchain

A blockchain, token standard, contract address and wallet provider have not yet been selected. No deposits or withdrawals are enabled in this version.

6. Distribution principles

Any future allocation may consider verified participation, eligible points, task completion and anti-fraud review. Final allocation rules and any conversion ratio must be published before distribution.

7. Roadmap

Nov 2026–Mar 2027: product foundation and backend planning.
Apr–Sep 2027: beta testing, ad-provider integration and community growth.
Oct 2027–Mar 2028: tokenomics, blockchain design and legal review.
Apr–Jun 2028: security audits, testnet and distribution simulations.
Jul–Dec 2028: target launch and eligible distribution, only if ready.

8. Security and compliance

The production service intends to use secure authentication, server-side reward verification, anti-abuse monitoring, secure key management and independent security review. Availability may vary by jurisdiction.

9. Sustainability

Potential funding sources may include compliant advertising and disclosed ecosystem partnerships. No fixed rewards or returns are guaranteed.

10. Risks

The project may be delayed, changed or cancelled. Token value, liquidity, exchange listing and financial returns are not guaranteed. Users should not spend money based on expected future rewards.

11. Contact and publication

Official website, Telegram and support contacts must be published and verified by the project team before launch.
