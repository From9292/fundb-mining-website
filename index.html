FundB Mining — Whitepaper

VERSION 1.0 · NOVEMBER 2026 · PROJECT PROPOSAL

The participation platform, points framework and long-term token vision — explained clearly, with current capabilities separated from future plans.

1. Introduction — A network built around participation

FundB is being developed as a mobile-first digital participation network. It is designed to bring account-based access, learning activities, verified missions, community participation and a transparent points record into one simple experience. Members should be able to understand what they can do, what has been verified and what remains under development.

This whitepaper distinguishes two different concepts: FundB Points, an in-app activity record, and a possible future FundB Token, which would be a separate blockchain asset only if the project completes the required technical, security, legal and operational work. Points are not tokens, and holding points does not currently create a right to receive cryptocurrency.

This is a development proposal, not a claim that every feature is live. The app currently supports account access and a mission-submission/review workflow. Point crediting, token distribution, mining rewards, transfers and withdrawals are not enabled.

2. FundB — Project principles

FundB aims to be accessible, transparent and fair. The project is guided by five principles: clear rules before participation; verification before any reward is recorded; no hidden charges to access a user's own eligible balance; protection of account and referral data; and honest status reporting when a feature is planned, testing or live.

FundB will not describe a timer, advertisement view, referral click or unverified task as proof that a reward has been earned. Any future points programme must be supported by reliable server-side records and published eligibility rules.

3. FundB Points — the in-app activity unit

FundB Points are intended to record eligible participation inside the FundB platform. They are not a cryptocurrency, do not run on a blockchain, cannot currently be transferred between accounts and cannot currently be withdrawn or redeemed for cash.

The app may display one simple planning illustration: 10 FundB Points ≈ $2. This is a visual reference only to help explain the concept. It is not a price, exchange rate, cash balance, guaranteed conversion, financial entitlement or promise of payment. The project has not established a redeemable monetary value for FundB Points.

Any future change to the points system will be announced before it takes effect. The platform will publish how points are earned, what evidence is required, how reviews work, whether limits apply and whether any points can qualify for a future programme.

4. A possible future FundB Token

FundB may evaluate a separate blockchain token in the future. No blockchain, token standard, token symbol, contract or mint address, decimal precision, total supply, allocation, liquidity plan or launch date has been finalized or announced. No FundB token is represented as minted or available today.

A future token, if approved, would be distinct from FundB Points. The existence of points would not by itself guarantee a token allocation. Any relationship between historic participation and a future distribution would require a published snapshot method, eligibility rules, anti-fraud review, supply budget and claim schedule before users could rely on it.

FundB will not claim that a token is audited, immutable, listed, liquid, withdrawable or verifiable on-chain unless those facts have actually been established and independently checked. A future token plan may be cancelled if it cannot be delivered safely or lawfully.

5. Proposed reward economics — controlled, not unlimited

A fixed reward promise that continues indefinitely can create an unlimited liability: as participation grows, the amount promised can exceed the resources available. FundB therefore intends to evaluate a capped-budget approach before activating any rewards.

One model under consideration separates any eligible pre-token activity from activity after a possible token launch:

  • Pre-launch review: if a token programme is ever approved, the project may take a dated snapshot of eligible, verified activity. The snapshot would be published with its rules and exclusions before any distribution decision.
  • One-time genesis allocation: any pre-launch distribution would be limited to a pre-announced budget. No conversion rate or allocation is promised in this whitepaper.
  • Season pools: after a possible launch, rewards could be allocated from fixed pools announced before each participation season. A participant's share would depend on their eligible verified activity compared with total eligible activity in that season.
  • Budget ceiling: the total amount distributed could never exceed the published allocation. If participation increases, individual shares may become smaller; the pool would not automatically expand.

This is a proposed framework, not an active reward programme. Season duration, pool sizes, distribution percentages, caps, vesting and any annual reduction schedule remain undecided. They will not be invented or implied in the app before a formal token design is approved.

6. FundB Mining — what mining means here

FundB Mining is planned as an app-based participation and activity system, not proof-of-work cryptocurrency mining. The app does not currently use a phone's processor to solve blockchain hashes, secure a blockchain or generate newly mined cryptocurrency. A mining screen or countdown must not be interpreted as evidence of blockchain mining.

Future sessions may use timed participation cycles, daily check-ins and verified activities. Before any session can credit points, the system must validate the session on the server, prevent replay and duplicate claims, apply clear daily limits and record an auditable result. The proposed 24-hour session experience is not a live earning guarantee.

7. Missions and verification

Missions are intended to reward genuine learning and useful participation, such as completing an educational activity, providing a meaningful response or contributing to an approved community task. Each mission should state its instructions, evidence requirements, eligibility and review criteria.

In the current release, members can submit mission evidence for review. A successful submission is saved as pending review; it does not mean the mission has been approved and no points are credited by this workflow. Authorized administrators can review submissions and reject those that do not meet the stated criteria. Point approval remains disabled until the secure reward ledger and approval process are implemented.

FundB intends to use consistent review criteria, duplicate detection and an audit trail. Automated, copied, misleading or fraudulent submissions may be rejected under published rules. Members should not submit passwords, private keys, seed phrases or unnecessary sensitive information as mission evidence.

8. Referrals and community integrity

Referral tools are intended to help members invite people to join the network. Referral relationships must be recorded by the server and protected against self-referrals, duplicate accounts, automated registrations and manipulation. A referral count alone does not create a points entitlement.

Any future referral reward must define what qualifies as an active referral, how long the qualifying activity must continue, the maximum reward or rate, and how fraudulent activity is handled. No referral percentage or points amount is active or promised in this release. Existing account and referral records must be preserved as the platform develops.

9. Distribution, wallet and withdrawals

The wallet area is a planned product feature. It is not currently a live blockchain wallet or a service for depositing, transferring or withdrawing FundB Points or tokens. Do not send cryptocurrency to an address or person claiming to represent a FundB wallet unless the project has published and verified an official process.

If a token is created in the future, the project must publish the supported blockchain and wallets, minimum claim amount, network fees, identity and regional requirements (if any), claim schedule, vesting conditions, distribution wallet and transaction verification process before withdrawals open.

FundB will not require users to share a seed phrase or private key. A legitimate wallet connection or withdrawal should require only the minimum information necessary, such as a public wallet address, and must never ask a user to disclose wallet recovery credentials.

10. Advertising and project sustainability

FundB may explore ordinary advertising, sponsorships or other clearly disclosed revenue sources to support hosting, moderation, development and operations. Advertising revenue is not guaranteed and does not automatically create a points entitlement.

Standard advertising services must be used in accordance with their policies. FundB will not ask members to click their own ads or interact with ads in ways prohibited by an advertising provider. Any future rewarded-ad feature must use a provider that expressly permits incentivized advertising and must verify completed events through trusted server callbacks. Ordinary ad impressions or clicks must not be presented as confirmed rewards.

11. Security, privacy and transparency

FundB uses Appwrite account authentication and cloud services for parts of its current platform. The project is being developed with authenticated access, server-side ownership checks and restricted administrative actions. These controls do not mean the entire platform has completed an independent security audit.

Before rewards or withdrawals are activated, FundB must implement and test a server-authoritative points ledger, immutable or tamper-evident audit records, strict access controls, rate limiting, abuse detection, secure backups, recovery procedures and appropriate monitoring. Sensitive actions should require authorization and be logged. Administrative access must be limited to explicitly approved accounts.

FundB will publish clear privacy information describing what data is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained and how users can request help. Members should use unique passwords, secure their email accounts and never share login codes or recovery credentials.

12. Roadmap — delivery in stages

  • Stage 1 — Account foundation: registration and sign-in, authenticated dashboard access, responsive navigation and clear product information.
  • Stage 2 — Verified participation: mission instructions, secure submissions, administrator review, user-visible status and a properly audited points ledger.
  • Stage 3 — Reward readiness: published points rules, fraud controls, transparent limits, operational funding and legal review before any points crediting is enabled.
  • Stage 4 — Community expansion: improved language support, support tools, community features and planned FundB Media experiences, introduced only when moderation and privacy controls are ready.
  • Stage 5 — Token feasibility: evaluate whether a token is technically, financially and legally appropriate. Only after that review may FundB publish token specifications, distribution, security verification and a realistic launch plan.

Any previously discussed target year, including 2028, is tentative and is not a launch commitment. FundB will communicate completed milestones and realistic next steps rather than promise dates it cannot guarantee.

13. Governance, changes and accountability

FundB's product rules, points policy and any future token policy should be versioned and dated. Material changes should be explained with the reason for the change and the effective date. If a reward model changes, users should be able to distinguish previously verified records from new rules. The project will not silently rewrite historical balances or referral relationships.

Before a future distribution, FundB should publish the relevant eligibility rules, budget, snapshot method, exclusions, vesting schedule, distribution wallet and a way for participants to independently verify completed transactions. Any claims about supply, locked liquidity, revoked authorities or third-party reviews must be backed by public evidence.

14. Risks and important limitations

  • Development risk: features may be delayed, changed or discontinued.
  • No guaranteed income: participation does not guarantee points, tokens, cash or profit.
  • Points are not money: the 10 points ≈ $2 display is illustrative only and is not a redeemable rate.
  • Token uncertainty: a FundB token may never be created; its supply, network, value and distribution are not established.
  • Reward uncertainty: any future pool share would depend on final rules and total eligible activity, and could be small or zero.
  • Security and operational risk: online accounts, software and any future blockchain system can experience outages, vulnerabilities or loss.
  • Regulatory risk: digital asset rules differ by country and may change. Access may be restricted where required by law.
  • Abuse controls: verified fraud or automated activity may be excluded under published programme rules.

Use only FundB's official app and communication channels for project updates. Be cautious of impersonators, fake support accounts and anyone promising guaranteed withdrawals or asking for a seed phrase. For assistance, use the contact details published in the official FundB app.
