How FundB Mining works

1. Create and verify an account

Register and sign in with an email address and password. Email verification and additional sign-in providers may be added after configuration.

2. Complete rewarded ads

Complete 10 eligible rewarded advertisements. The ad provider verifies each completion; ordinary ads must not be incentivized.

3. Start a 24-hour session

A verified session runs for 24 hours. The server records the start and end times so closing the app does not erase progress.

4. Earn points and complete tasks

Eligible activity is recorded in a secure ledger. Tasks are subject to verification and published rules.

5. Token distribution preparation

Before any token distribution, the project will publish eligibility, anti-fraud rules and the points-to-token formula. Points do not guarantee tokens.

FUND-B MINING & MEDIA — PROJECT WHITEPAPER

VERSION 2.2 · NOVEMBER 2026 · PROPOSAL

1. Executive summary

FundB is a proposed global participation ecosystem combining FundB Mining, FUNDB and FundB Media. The vision is to bring people together to learn, create, communicate, play skill-based games and participate in community activities through one accessible mobile-first platform.

2. Mission and product

FundB Mining is intended to support verified participation activities, learning tasks and community engagement. FundB Media is planned as a multilingual social space for profiles, messaging, friendships, community groups, educational content, games, live streaming and audio/video conversations. Features will be released in stages after safety, privacy and reliability testing.

3. FUNDB token and reference display

FUNDB is currently deployed only on BNB Smart Chain testnet. The app's illustrative display rate of $0.60 per FUNDB is a design reference only, not a market price, redemption rate, investment value or claim against the project. Testnet tokens cannot be exchanged for cash or cryptocurrency. Any future token distribution would require a confirmed launch, published eligibility rules and a verified allocation process; no allocation is guaranteed.

4. Proposed community airdrop

The project intends to explore a broad community airdrop for eligible users if a token is successfully created and launched. Potential eligibility factors may include verified account status, genuine participation, anti-fraud checks, regional availability and published campaign rules. No user is guaranteed an allocation, minimum amount, token price, exchange listing or profit. The final snapshot date, allocation formula, exclusions, appeal process and distribution schedule must be announced before any airdrop.

5. Reward programme design

Potential activities include verified mining sessions, daily tasks, quizzes, completed game levels, community contributions and eligible streaming participation. Proposed examples in the product concept include 20 FUNDB for a verified game level and 35 FUNDB for an eligible one-hour streaming session. These are proposed rates only; they are not active reward entitlements until server-side verification, funding, anti-abuse limits and final terms are implemented.

6. FundB Media and safety

The planned media service will support language selection, community posts, direct messages, friend connections, moderated groups, games, creator content, livestreams and audio/video calls. AI translation may be offered where technically available. Users must be able to report, block and mute others. Age-appropriate safeguards, content moderation, privacy controls and clear community rules are required before public launch.

7. Revenue and sustainability

Potential revenue sources include non-incentivized advertising placements that comply with each ad network's policies, clearly labelled sponsored posts and brand partnerships, optional premium memberships, creator subscriptions, virtual gifts or tips where lawful, and ticketed online events. Users must never be required to pay to access promised airdrops. Ordinary AdSense ads must not be rewarded or made conditional on earning points. Revenue sharing, fees and creator payouts will require published terms and payment-provider integration.

8. Technology and security

The current community testnet interface uses Appwrite for email/password authentication and saved cloud profiles, with the website deployed through Cloudflare Pages. Mining rewards, daily claims, ad rewards, referral attribution, wallet transfers and withdrawals are not active. Production requires server-authoritative point ledgers, strict database permissions, rate limits, anti-bot controls, verified rewarded-ad callbacks, secure messaging, moderation tools, backups and independent security testing. Client-side balances and browser timers are not valid proof of rewards.

9. Token and distribution design

The blockchain, token standard, supply, contract address, vesting, liquidity arrangements and legal structure have not been finalized. These details must be independently reviewed and published before any token sale or airdrop. No deposits or withdrawals are enabled in this version.

10. Roadmap

Phase 1: app identity, FUNDB token display, whitepaper, support and community testnet.
Phase 2: Appwrite profiles, server ledger, verified referrals, moderation and language support.
Phase 3: compliant advertising, creator tools, real-time chat and games.
Phase 4: streaming, calls, AI translation, monetization and security review.
Phase 5: token design, legal review, audits and possible distribution. The 2028 date is a target only and may change.

11. Risks and important disclosures

The project may be delayed, changed or cancelled. Participation points may never convert into a token. Token value, liquidity, listing, revenue share, airdrop size and financial returns are not guaranteed. Do not pay anyone claiming they can guarantee FundB rewards or airdrop allocation. Users should not spend money based on anticipated future rewards.
