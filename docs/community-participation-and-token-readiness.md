# FundB Community Participation and Token Readiness Policy

**Status:** Draft for community review — not a promise of rewards, token allocation, listing, income, or launch.  
**Current environment:** BNB Smart Chain Chapel testnet only.  
**Mainnet:** Not deployed, not scheduled, and not authorized by this document.

## 1. Purpose

FundB Mining is being prepared as a community participation and learning project. The first priority is a safe, understandable product with transparent rules. Community participation may be recorded while the project is tested, but participation does not automatically create a right to money, cryptocurrency, or a future token allocation.

## 2. Current token test deployment

The current test deployment is:

- Network: BNB Smart Chain Chapel testnet (chain ID 97)
- Token name: FundB
- Symbol: FUNDB
- Decimals: 18
- Fixed test supply: 10,000,000,000 FUNDB
- Test contract: `0x6903A593920F9aA15D0673048A4D4eb40c5eE827`
- Test explorer: https://testnet.bscscan.com/address/0x6903A593920F9aA15D0673048A4D4eb40c5eE827

Testnet tokens are for software testing only. They have no promised or established monetary value and must not be sold or represented as redeemable assets.

The current test deployment assigned its full supply to the nominated test receiving wallet. This is a technical test arrangement, not a proposed public token allocation or final tokenomics plan. Any future mainnet supply, distribution, vesting, treasury, team, community, liquidity, or reserve allocation must be separately designed, disclosed, reviewed, and approved before a mainnet deployment.

## 3. FundB Points and participation

Until separate production rules and services are approved:

- FundB Points are internal participation records in the preview app, not FUNDB tokens, cash, shares, deposits, or a debt owed to a user.
- Points are not currently withdrawable, transferable, redeemable, or guaranteed to convert into tokens.
- A task, referral, ad view, quiz, game, or other activity must not be described as guaranteeing a future airdrop.
- Any future eligibility programme must publish its dates, qualifying activities, geographic restrictions (if any), exclusions, anti-abuse rules, review process, and dispute process before it begins.
- No user should pay money, connect a wallet, disclose a recovery phrase, or click an advertisement to qualify for a promised allocation.

If a future programme offers rewards for advertising, it must use an advertising provider and rewarded format that explicitly permit incentivized activity, with server-side verification. Ordinary AdSense ads must never be incentivized or require clicks.

## 4. Participation integrity and account protection

Before real rewards are enabled, the project must implement and test:

- Server-authoritative points and activity records; never trust browser-only balances.
- An append-only, auditable reward ledger with unique transaction identifiers and idempotency.
- Verified referral relationships, duplicate-account controls, rate limits, and abuse review.
- Clear user notices, support contact, complaint handling, and correction procedures.
- Least-privilege access for administrators, secure secret storage, backups, and incident response.
- Privacy notice, terms of use, data retention rules, and appropriate legal review for the countries served.

A user's existing account, points history, and referral records must not be reset as part of token-readiness work.

## 5. Wallet and network safety

The app may first add read-only testnet wallet connection and balance display. It must clearly show the selected network and distinguish testnet from mainnet.

- Never request or collect a user's seed phrase or private key.
- Never ask a user to send real assets to a testnet contract.
- Do not enable token transfers, approvals, swaps, deposits, or withdrawals in the preview.
- Any future wallet transaction must explain the network, asset, destination, amount, and fees before the user approves it in their own wallet.
- Mainnet configuration must remain disabled until separately reviewed and explicitly approved.

## 6. Mainnet launch gates

A mainnet launch must not proceed until all of the following are completed:

1. A final whitepaper and tokenomics schedule, including the full supply allocation, vesting, unlocks, treasury controls, and any liquidity plan.
2. Independent review of the final contract source, compiler settings, deployment process, and verified source publication.
3. Legal and regulatory review for the jurisdictions in which the project will operate and market the token.
4. Published participation and eligibility rules, including anti-fraud and appeal procedures.
5. Production security review of authentication, database permissions, reward ledger, referral logic, advertising callbacks, admin controls, and wallet flows.
6. End-to-end testing, backup and recovery exercises, monitoring, support readiness, and a public incident-response process.
7. A documented go/no-go decision by the project owner. Passing technical tests alone does not authorize launch.

No launch date, exchange listing, price, profit, or guaranteed allocation is promised.

## 7. Change control

This policy is a draft. Changes to tokenomics, eligibility, reward rates, or launch conditions must be versioned and announced before they affect participants. Material changes must not be applied retroactively to remove already-confirmed rights under any separately published programme.

## 8. Current user-facing status

Until the launch gates are met, FundB Mining should remain labelled as a preview/community test. Mining rewards, ad rewards, withdrawals, token conversion, and guaranteed airdrops must remain disabled. The app may invite users to explore features and participate in clearly labelled testing without implying that they are earning money or guaranteed tokens.
