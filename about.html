About FundB Mining

FundB Mining is a community participation project exploring learning activities, community features and a proposed FundB points ecosystem. It is accessible as a web preview while services are being developed.

Current preview status

Accounts and profiles are connected to cloud authentication. The app currently does not issue redeemable rewards. Mining, ad rewards, daily claims, transfers and withdrawals are disabled. FundB Points shown in the preview are not cash or cryptocurrency and have no established monetary value.

How participation is planned

  • Register with an email address and password.
  • Explore learning and community features.
  • Account-to-account referral linking is active; referral bonuses are not active. Linking an account does not earn points or money.
  • Future rewards, if launched, will require published rules, server-side verification, abuse prevention and funding.

Frequently asked questions

Is FundB Mining paying users now?

No. Reward earning and withdrawals are not active in this preview.

Are FundB Points cryptocurrency?

No. They are preview participation points and are not currently transferable or redeemable.

Do I need to pay or deposit to participate?

No payment or deposit is required for the current preview. Do not pay anyone who claims to unlock guaranteed rewards or an airdrop.

Are ads active?

Rewarded-ad integration is not active. The preview does not count ad views as rewards.
