FundB Mining & Media — Privacy Notice

Preview draft: This is an interim privacy notice, not legal advice or a legal-compliance certification. It needs review and completion before a public earning or financial launch.

Information used

Registration account information such as email and display name is handled by Appwrite authentication. The preview profile may store a user ID, points balance, referral code, referral attribution and referral count. Support communications may include information you choose to provide.

Purpose

Information is used to sign in, load the preview profile, display referral information and respond to support requests. The transaction-history endpoint is intended to read only a user's own ledger entries.

Preview features and payments

Mining rewards, rewarded-ad credits, token issuance, deposits, transfers, withdrawals and cash conversion are not enabled. Preview points are not money and have no guaranteed value.

Providers and security

FundB uses Appwrite for authentication and backend profile storage and loads the Appwrite SDK from a third-party CDN. Provider terms and privacy notices may also apply. FundB should review security, retention and provider configuration before launch.

Referrals

Referral codes may record account relationships. Attribution is intended to be one-time and to prevent self-referrals. Referral reward payments are not active.

Requests and retention

A verified self-service account deletion process is not yet available. To request access, correction or deletion, contact luckyutu9@gmail.com. Requests are subject to identity verification and applicable legal retention obligations.

Children

The service is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law to consent to an online service.

Contact

Privacy questions: luckyutu9@gmail.com or the official FundB Telegram community. Never email passwords, verification codes or wallet recovery phrases.
