This notice describes how FundB Mining's preview website handles information. The preview is not a fully launched rewards service.

Advertising integration and ad tracking are not currently active in this preview. If advertising is introduced, this notice will be updated to identify relevant providers, data use, consent controls where required and applicable choices. Standard advertising is not a points-reward mechanism.

Information is used to operate account sign-in and profile features, respond to support requests, maintain security and improve the service. We do not claim that this preview has a complete production privacy or compliance program.

Third parties

Retention and your choices

Retention, deletion and access procedures are still being formalized. Contact us to request assistance with account-related privacy questions. Applicable local law may provide additional rights.

This preview is not designed for children under 13, or the higher minimum age where local law requires it. Do not use the service if you do not meet the applicable minimum age.
