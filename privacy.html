FundB Mining & Media — Privacy Notice

About this notice: This document explains the information used by the current FundB service. It is an interim notice, not legal advice or a legal-compliance certification. It should be reviewed and updated as new services and jurisdictions are supported.

1. Information we use

When you register or sign in, Appwrite authentication processes your email address, account identifier and display name. The FundB profile service may store your account ID, internal community balance, referral code, referral attribution and referral count. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. Technical service logs may contain request and security information needed to operate and protect the platform.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, load and maintain profiles, attribute referrals, receive mission submissions, provide support, protect the service and investigate abuse. Account-specific profile and transaction information is intended to be restricted to the authenticated account owner and authorized service processes.

3. Community balances, rewards and payments

Internal FUNDB community units are not cash or a live token. Mining rewards, rewarded-ad credits, token issuance, deposits, transfers, withdrawals and cash conversion are not currently active. The illustrative reference of 5 FUNDB = $3 is not a market price or a promise of payment.

4. Service providers and advertising

FundB uses Appwrite for authentication and backend profile services and loads its software development tools from third-party providers. The website includes Google AdSense publisher code. If Google serves advertisements, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, cookies or related information under their own policies and settings. Standard ads are not linked to FUNDB rewards. Review the relevant provider privacy notices and settings.

5. Referrals and submissions

Referral codes may be used to record which account introduced a new member. Mission submissions and support messages are retained as needed to review contributions, respond to requests and protect the service.

6. Security and retention

FundB uses access controls and authenticated service requests, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, support, security, dispute handling and legal obligations.

7. Your requests

A verified self-service account deletion process is not currently available. To request access, correction or deletion, contact luckyutu9@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership and may retain information where required by law or needed to resolve security or legal issues.

8. International users and children

FundB is intended for eligible users in supported locations. Information may be processed by service providers in countries other than your own, subject to provider safeguards and applicable law. The service is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law to consent to an online service.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as FundB adds services, providers and supported locations. Material changes will be communicated through official FundB channels.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: luckyutu9@gmail.com or the official FundB Telegram community. Never email passwords, verification codes or wallet recovery phrases.
