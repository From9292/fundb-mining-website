FundB Mining & Media — Privacy Notice

About this notice: This document explains the information used by the current FundB service. It is an interim notice, not legal advice or a legal-compliance certification. It should be reviewed and updated as new services and jurisdictions are supported.

1. Information we use

When you register or sign in, Appwrite authentication processes your email address, account identifier and display name. The FundB profile service may store your account ID, internal community balance, referral code, referral attribution and referral count. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. Technical service logs may contain request and security information needed to operate and protect the platform.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, load and maintain profiles, attribute referrals, receive mission submissions, provide support, protect the service and investigate misuse. FundB follows a one-person, one-account participation principle. If account-integrity checks are introduced, the privacy notice will describe the relevant technical signals and retention before collection begins. A shared device alone will not be treated as proof that accounts belong to one person. Account-specific profile and transaction information is intended to be restricted to the authenticated account owner and authorized service processes.

3. Testnet and participation records

The BNB Smart Chain testnet token is used for development testing and is not the live FUNDB mainnet token. FundB does not currently issue on-chain testnet tokens to app users. The service may retain verified account and participation records for security, review and possible consideration under a future distribution programme. Retention of a record does not guarantee an airdrop, allocation or token conversion. Any future programme will have separately published eligibility and privacy information.

4. Community balances, rewards and payments

Internal FUNDB community units are not cash and do not have an established market price. Mining rewards, rewarded-ad credits, token issuance, deposits, transfers, withdrawals and cash conversion are not currently active.

5. Service providers, cookies and advertising

FundB uses Appwrite for authentication and backend profile services and loads software development tools from third-party providers. The website includes Google AdSense publisher code. If Google serves ads, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver, measure and protect advertising, subject to the settings and consent requirements that apply to the visitor. Third parties may place and read cookies in a visitor’s browser, or use web beacons and IP addresses, as a result of ad serving on this website.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services. Visitors can review Google ad settings and their browser cookie controls. Where required, FundB will use an appropriate consent mechanism before serving personalized advertising. Standard website ads are not linked to FUNDB balances or participation rewards, and FundB never asks users to click ads.

Publisher verification code being present does not mean Google has approved the site or that ads are being served. Google and other providers process information under their own privacy notices and applicable settings.

6. Referrals and submissions

Referral codes may be used to record which account introduced a new member. Mission submissions and support messages are retained as needed to review contributions, respond to requests and protect the service.

7. Security and retention

FundB uses access controls and authenticated service requests, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. If device or registration signals are introduced to protect account integrity, FundB will update this notice to explain what is collected, why it is needed and how long it is retained before those signals are used. Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, support, security, dispute handling and legal obligations.

8. Your requests

A verified self-service account deletion process is not currently available. To request access, correction or deletion, contact luckyutu9@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership and may retain information where required by law or needed to resolve security or legal issues.

9. International users and children

FundB is intended for eligible users in supported locations. Information may be processed by service providers in countries other than your own, subject to provider safeguards and applicable law. The service is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law to consent to an online service.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as FundB adds services, providers and supported locations. Material changes will be communicated through official FundB channels.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: luckyutu9@gmail.com or the official FundB Telegram community. Never email passwords, verification codes or wallet recovery phrases.
