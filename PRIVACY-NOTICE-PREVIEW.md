# FundB Mining & Media — Privacy Notice (Preview)

**Draft for review; not legal advice.** This notice describes the current FundB community-preview website, not services that have not yet been launched. Have qualified counsel review it before public launch and adapt it to the jurisdictions where the service operates.

## Information currently used
- Account information provided during registration, including display name and email address, is processed by Appwrite authentication.
- The preview profile service may store a user identifier, FundB points balance (currently not earnable through active reward mechanisms), referral code, referral attribution and referral count.
- Support messages or information users voluntarily send through the support channels may contain personal information.

## Purpose
Account data is used to sign in, load the preview profile, display the referral code and attributed referral status, and provide support. The current transaction-history endpoint is intended to read only a user's own ledger entries; it does not issue rewards or enable financial activity.

## Rewards and payments
Mining rewards, rewarded-ad credits, token issuance, deposits, transfers, withdrawals and cash conversion are not enabled in this preview. Points displayed in the preview are not money and have no guaranteed value.

## Providers and security
FundB uses Appwrite for authentication and backend profile storage. The website also loads the Appwrite SDK from a third-party CDN. Providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy notices. FundB should restrict backend access, avoid sharing credentials, and review provider configuration and retention practices before launch.

## Referrals
Referral codes may be used to record a relationship between accounts. Existing attribution is intended to be one-time and protected against self-referral. Referral relationships do not currently generate reward payments.

## Retention, rights and requests
The preview does not yet offer a verified self-service account-deletion workflow. Users may request help, access, correction or deletion by contacting the support email listed in the app. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law, security verification, and necessary records retention.

## Children
The preview is not designed for children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law to consent to an online service. Do not knowingly register a child who is not legally permitted to use the service.

## Changes and contact
This draft may be updated as the service evolves. Contact **luckyutu9@gmail.com** or the official Telegram community linked in the app for privacy questions. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or payment credentials by email.

**Before launch:** confirm the data inventory, retention periods, deletion process, applicable legal basis, international transfer safeguards, age policy and provider contracts, then publish a reviewed final notice.
